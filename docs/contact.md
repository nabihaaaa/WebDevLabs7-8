# Contact

Need help or have a question? 📩  
PaperBeam offers several ways to connect with our team and community.

<!-- reach -->
## Ways to Reach Us

Choose the option that best fits your needs:

- **Email Support** — for general questions and detailed inquiries  
- **Live Chat** — for quick, real-time assistance  
- **Community Forum** — for discussions, tips, and shared solutions  

<!-- avail -->
## Availability📱

Our support team is available:

- **Monday to Friday**  
- **9:00 AM – 5:00 PM**  
- **Response time:** typically within 24 hours  

<!-- what channels are best for -->
## Support Channels

| Channel | Best For |
|---------|----------|
| Email | General inquiries and support |
| Live Chat | Fast responses and urgent help |
| Forum | Community discussions and troubleshooting |

::: tip
Check the community forum first — many common questions already have helpful answers.
:::

::: warning
For your security, avoid sharing sensitive information such as passwords or API keys when contacting support.
:::
